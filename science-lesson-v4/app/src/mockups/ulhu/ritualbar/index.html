The 2-week bar under each ritual

Item 1U.13. Three ways to show the last 14 days under a ritual tablet. Same pretend data in each: Read (school days), Floss (every day) and Call a friend (every 3 days). Pick a letter, or say what to mix.

Today: a 4-pixel line of 14 dashes, hard to read and no days shown.

B1 Two weeks of pebbles

14 round pebbles, last week and this week with a gap between them, a day letter under each. Glowing green = done, red ring = missed, amber = a forgiven miss, ice = day off, a small dash = not due that day. Today has a white ring.

B2 The tide line

One wavy line across 14 days that rises on done days and sinks on misses, like a tide. Higher = a better run. Small dots mark each day; the numbers say this week against last week.

B3 Carved ledge

The bottom edge of the stone tablet becomes a carved ledge with 14 sockets. A done day holds a small polished green stone that stands up out of the ledge; misses are an empty socket rimmed red. Feels part of the tablet rather than a chart.
