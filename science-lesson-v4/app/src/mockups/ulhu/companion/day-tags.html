Day tags: where do they live?

You tag your own heavy days (Marking, Parents' evening, INSET, Report writing, Trip, Cover) and Ill days. Heavy tags change no rules; they only show on the term page. Ill turns the moon dark and curses cost no health. Ulhu never suggests a tag. Three places they could live. Pick a letter (or mix).

A. Hold the moon

Tap the moon still changes its phase. Hold it and a sheet rises: this week's days on top, tap a day, then tap its tags. Nothing new on the main screen.
