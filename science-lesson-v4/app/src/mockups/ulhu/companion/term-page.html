Who I'm becoming, and the chapter windows

The term page (one per half-term act). The identities you gave your habits (Scholar, Steadfast, Nourished and the rest) are stained-glass figures that light pane by pane, one pane per vote, fully lit at 30. Under them: "on your better days, these came first", from simple counting. Your tagged heavy days are listed and change nothing. Then the act's chapters: one window per school week in blue (blessings), red (curses) and gold (rituals), sealed when High Vespers closes it. Three layouts; pick one, or say which bits to mix.
