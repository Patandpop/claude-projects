Ulhu's overnight adventure, round 2

Your picks: 1C he leaves a note (with a better note and a better lantern), the card under the banner made far better (the grey-night tone of 2C kept), and 3B cabinets per act that open, with finds that give notes you piece together into one torn page per act. Three versions of each to choose from. Tap things: notes, nets, cabinets, finds.

1. While he is away: the lantern and the note

After Vespers. The ledge is empty, rings spread where he dived, and the lantern lights the note. A new line each night. Tap a note to read it close up; "next night" shows another.

2. The morning: the find arrives under the banner

From 6 am he is back, a little wet. The find arrives under the banner. Switch between a good night and a grey night (after a bad day: smaller, odder, no runes, never a cost).

3. Where finds live: a cabinet for each act

A hall of seven cabinets, one per half-term act. Past acts are shut, this act glows, later ones are dusty with barnacles. Tap a cabinet: its doors swing open. Tap a find: it gives you its note, a torn piece of one page. Each act's eight pieces make one page, the story of that act, told without ever quite explaining it.

tap a find
The page is whole. It goes in the back of the cabinet, and the act's window in the term page gets one more pane of light.
