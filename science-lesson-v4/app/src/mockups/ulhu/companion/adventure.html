Ulhu's overnight adventure

After Vespers he sinks into the deep. The app works exactly as normal while he is away (ticks, new habits, Vespers). From 6 am he is back with one find: mostly an odd object for the Sanctum shelf or a lore line, now and then a few runes. After a bad day he still goes and brings back something small or strange; it never costs anything. Three ways to show each part. Pick a letter per row (for example "1B, 2A, 3C").
