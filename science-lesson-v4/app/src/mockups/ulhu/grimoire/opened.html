The Grimoire, opened

Item 1U.10. When the tentacles drag you in and the book opens, it lands on your day in stained glass (the Oracle glass): the morning star, the middle mark and the night moon, in two rings of glass round the gold eye. Pretend day: most rituals kept, one curse. Pick a letter.

O1 One rose across the spread

A single round window laid over both pages, the gold eye on the gutter. The inner ring is today's three marks (star at the top, middle mark lower left, moon lower right); the outer ring is the day's hours, lit where you kept something, dark red where you slipped.

O2 Two pages, two rings

Left page: the inner ring with the star, the middle mark and the moon round the gold eye. Right page: the outer ring opened out as a tall lancet window, morning at the top, night at the bottom, one pane per ritual or omen.

O3 Three medallions down the gutter

Three round glass medallions stacked on the gutter, each a small two-ring window: star (morning), middle mark (day), moon (night), with the gold eye in the middle one. The pages either side hold the day's rituals in ink, ticked.

Glass colours as in the Oracle: honey star = most or all kept, ember = some kept, pale moon = none, cursed purple = a slip.
