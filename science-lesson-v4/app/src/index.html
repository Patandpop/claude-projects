    Settings

    This PC's evening timer

    For the home PC only. With ActivityWatch running on this PC, the app counts what you work on (Emails, Building lessons 9AS, ...) and sends only minutes per job and class. Window names never leave this PC. Little Ulhu says what he is counting; after 7pm he only counts.

    Background jobs

    These run on the planner while you carry on. A pill at the top shows how each one is going.

    Notifications

    For this device. Nothing before 7am or after 7pm. The morning one comes at 7am on school days only, and only when a lesson today is not built (class codes and lesson titles, never names). Ulhu's nudge is at most one a day.
