Positives board

Pop the board out once. It floats above everything, including a full-screen PowerPoint, and keeps updating from your phone. Drag its corner in as far as it goes. The text redraws sharp at any size.

Silences are judged by you, not a microphone: while your phone's Talking button is on, the board shows the sad face and those seconds count as not silent.
