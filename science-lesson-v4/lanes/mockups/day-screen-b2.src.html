Day screen B, with soul: B1, B2 or B3?

All three are your timeline (B) in the planner's own look: navy and gold, Century Gothic, dark by default (Light is the switch, top right). Every class is in its planner colour, the buttons carry colour (gold Start and Save, green Yes, Move), and the running timer sits as a bar fixed at the top while it runs; its Start buttons stay in the page. Ulhu sits on the workload line, calm when the day fits, stirring when it is over. They differ in how the class colour is used. B3 adds one small, optional Ulhu touch to the to-do list.

B1. Navy and gold

Lessons are solid blocks in their class colour, like the planner's week grid. The running bar is navy with a gold edge.
