    How to run it

    1. Read it together, one voice, on your three, two, one.
    2. One turn each: press Space and the highlight walks round the phrase by itself. Point at who's next. Space pauses.
    3. Words vanish. Each time, the whole phrase gets said again, gaps included. The chip at the top right says how.
    4. First letters only, then write it down using them.
    5. Blank board. Say it together, then pick a number for someone solo.
    6. Write it from memory, then check. Tick each word, circle the key words they missed.

    Hover the mouse over a gap to show that word faintly. Keys: Space next or start/pause the walk, arrows back and forward, R replays, V changes the voice, P picks a number, Esc goes back to the phrase.
