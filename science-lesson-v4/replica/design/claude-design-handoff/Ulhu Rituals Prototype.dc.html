WHAT CHANGED FROM TODAY'S APP
One home for the game. The classroom timer controls moved off this screen. Ulhu, the doubloons, the bars and his line now sit in one banner that takes about a quarter of the screen.
No more "Archive" on every row. Each row now shows what matters: a 46 px tick, the length, the kind, the streak and the pay. Editing moved into the group header (✎) and the forms.
The Undo line is a real line. It shows the last action and how much it changed your doubloons or health. It never covers anything.
Every list folds. 7 marking cards now take less space than 2 did, in a two-column grid. The two boss cards that showed every stage at once now fold.
Numbers in Atkinson Hyperlegible. 2, 5 and 8 can't be confused. The pixel font is only used for art, and Grenze Gotisch is used for the runic headings.
Actions are in the thumb zone. The add buttons, the day-off button and the Den views sit in the dock at the bottom. Forms open in place of the list, not on top of it.
