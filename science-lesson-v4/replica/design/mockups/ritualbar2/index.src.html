The 2-week bar, in glass

Round two of 1U.13, from B1. Under each real ritual tablet, the last two weeks as a strip of stained glass: one leaded pane per day, one or two breaks in each pane, nothing laid on top. No more "not due" dashes: a ritual that is due every few days gets ONE pane across those days, lit once it is done, and split in two if you did it twice. A school-day ritual's Friday pane stretches over the weekend. Same pretend data in every card. Pick a letter, or mix.
