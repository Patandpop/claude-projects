Level Up, round three

Three completely different ideas. Each plays once on load; Replay runs it again. The heal only arrives as a quiet last beat, after the big moment has landed.

LU-XThe Breach

Ulhu rises from the deep, bursts through the surface into the night, and the stars join up into his new level.

LU-YThe Drowned Bell

The temple bell tolls three times, shaking the hall, then pours itself as molten gold into the mould of his new number.

LU-ZThe Colossus

Ulhu swells to a giant over a tiny town, eyes blazing, then shrinks back with his new number branded on his brow.

Heal beat: X and Z tick a small health bar to full in the corner; Y adds a soft green glow behind Ulhu as well.
