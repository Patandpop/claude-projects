Focus crafts: Ulhu makes something while you focus

You start a focus session; Ulhu sits down and makes something. Leaving the app PAUSES him: he dozes with his tool in hand and picks up exactly where he stopped when you come back. Nothing ever crumbles. Each craft has variations so it is not the same thing every time (shown under each card). Drag the slider to see each craft grow from start to finished, and press "Leave the app" to see the paused look. Pick the crafts you like (several is fine) and the ones to drop.
