Ulhu's runes come home

FA2-a gold dawn, three ways for the lost runes to come back. Ulhu faints but stays alive; each ritual lights one stained-glass star, and the runes float down to him in bubbles. Every bubble that reaches him gives back health and colour, lifts him a little and makes him glow, until he is standing with full health. A faint still costs: today's three rituals bring back one quarter of the lost runes (78 of 310), and the next quarter comes with tomorrow's three.
