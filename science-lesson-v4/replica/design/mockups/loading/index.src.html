Ulhu loading screens

Three scenes, two takes each. Every scene is one tiny canvas and about thirty lines of drawing, so any of them can be the real loading screen.

L1 Spinning round

L1a Turntable

He turns on the spot like a coin, back to front, while a ring of sea light circles his feet.

Ulhu is turning the tide

L1b The whirl

Wider framing: he tumbles head over tentacles in a whirlpool, his five runes orbiting.

Down the whirl we go

L2 Reading a scroll

L2a Rune by rune

He holds the scroll wide and his eyes follow the line; each rune glows as he reads it, then the scroll flashes and starts again.

Reading the old words

L2b The runes wake

A hanging scroll unrolls by candlelight and its runes lift off the page and drift up as sigils.

The runes are waking

L3 Stirring a cauldron

L3a The stir

He stands behind the pot and stirs in slow circles; bubbles pop and steam curls up.

Something is brewing

L3b Sigil bubbles

Close up: only his eyes peek over the rim. Big bubbles rise and burst into glowing sigils, firelight flickering on the dark.

Bubbles into sigils
