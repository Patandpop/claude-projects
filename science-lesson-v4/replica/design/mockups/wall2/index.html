The Wall and the Paper Golems

Round 2 mock-ups, 29 Oct. Section 1 is three ways to show how far ahead the lessons are built. Section 2 is the marking golems. Tap the pieces.

1. Keeping ahead

One piece per lesson in the next 2 weeks (2 to 13 Nov). A piece is complete when the lesson is built AND homework is set. The whole next week done is "keeping ahead"; both weeks done pays the lesson-ahead bonus once. Tap a gap to see which lesson it is (in the planner it jumps there).

W-AThe Moon Window

Stained glass: each lesson is a moon in two halves, the class colour lights when built, the silver half when homework is set.

Tap a moon.

W-BThe Sea Wall

One stone per lesson, days along the top, class names written in the gaps; homework is the mortar.

Tap a stone or a gap.

W-CThe Tide Gauge

The water mark shows how many school days ahead are fully done, with carved marks at 1 and 2 weeks.

Tap a day stone, or a gap below.

2. Paper golems

Summoned through the boss circle when the planner suggests it. Each marking sitting tears off a chunk of paper, red ticks spread and one health block breaks. Beating one gives runes and a small trophy for the Sanctum.

Class theme

He picks one of 8 themes per class. Tap to try a theme on every golem; tap again to go back to each card's own.

Health: 2 sittings per class per term by default (changeable per class). PAG golems: finish-by is one week after the practical.
