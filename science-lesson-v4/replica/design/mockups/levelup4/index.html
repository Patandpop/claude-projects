The Breach, more triumphant

Three takes on LU-X. Same story every time: up from the deep, through the surface, stars join into his level. Each one turns the triumph up a different way. The heal stays a quiet corner beat at the end.

LU-X1The Hero Burst

A gold-lit column of water lifts him high, tentacles raised, and the stars snap into his level with a flash.

LU-X2The Sky Answers

Stars rush in from every edge, aurora curtains fall, and his big new number pulses three times like a fanfare.

LU-X3The Crowning

A ring of stars settles on his head as a crown, the sea glows beneath him, and runes burst like fireworks.

All three end with the same small flask and health bar ticking to full, top left, after the big moment.
