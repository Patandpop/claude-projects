The dice result: who won

Three ways to show the end of a haggle. Tap Win or Lose under each one to play it. The words are only ideas: DR1 IA! / FHTAGN, DR2 YIELDED / CLAIMED, DR3 Y'HAH / N'GHA. You can swap them.

DR1 Face-off

A light shaft drops on the winner, who rises up and glows. The loser sinks into the dark. The loser's dice fall off the table.

DR2 The circle chooses

The green summoning circle on the table flares up and fires a beam at the winner. On a loss it burns red and the beam goes to the keeper.

DR3 The tide takes the loser

Sea water floods the loser's side of the table, with bubbles (the same look as the Low tide way in). The winner stays dry.
