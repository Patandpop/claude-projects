Wall, Golems and Inscriptions

Three pieces for the Teaching area and the add forms. Use the buttons above each one to switch between options.

1 · Teaching area, top

The sea wall

One stone per lesson in the next two weeks. Built lessons are stones, unbuilt ones are gaps the sea breaks through. Homework is the pale mortar. Tap a gap.

Paper golems
