Ulhu's runes come home

FA2-a gold dawn, three ways for the lost runes to come back. Ulhu faints but stays alive; each ritual lights one many-coloured stained-glass star, piece by piece, and his rune tablets (the same ones he spends in the app) float down to him in bubbles. Every bubble that reaches him gives back health and colour, lifts him a little and makes him glow, until he is standing with full health. A faint still costs: today's three rituals bring back one quarter of the lost runes (78 of 310), and the next quarter comes with tomorrow's three.
