The dice game: the water

Item 1U.6. Seen from above, over the table, the keeper across from you. Flat pixel water, not 3D. Each panel loops: a die is lost and taken, then the game ends and the water takes the loser. Tap Win or Lose to see either ending. Pick a letter (or mix), and pick the losing word at the bottom.

W1 The tide sweep

A band of water rolls across the table from the side, like the tide coming in. A lost die is lifted off the table and carried out with it. At the end the tide rolls back in, higher, and carries the loser off the edge.

W2 The tentacle in the pool

Water seeps up through the cracks of the table and pools round the lost die; a tentacle rises out of the pool, wraps it and pulls it under with a swirl. At the end the pool floods the whole table and the tentacle drags the loser down.

W3 The breaking wave

A wave breaks over the far edge of the table (from behind the keeper when you lose a die, from your side when the keeper does) and a pale hand reaches out of the foam for the die. At the end a big wave breaks over the loser: over the keeper, or straight at you, filling the screen.

The end words

Shown big, then the item's new price in glowing runes. The win word is fixed. Pick the losing word (ENGULFT stays if you do not pick).
