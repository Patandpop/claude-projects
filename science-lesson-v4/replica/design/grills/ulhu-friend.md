# Grilling: Goal 4, Ulhu for his friend (not a teacher)

Grilled 29 Oct 2026 with Patryk, 5 rounds. Status: ready to build, once the installed-app version exists (see "Depends on").

## Decisions

**What it is.** She saw Patryk's Ulhu, loves it, and wants the two of them to have it together: see each other, motivate each other, depend on each other. The together layer is the heart of the goal. Aim: far better than Habitica.

**Depends on.** The installed-app enabler in FABLE-WORKPLAN.md "Big goals". There is no Google Sheets copy route. She gets the app, and updates come with it.

**Build order.**
1. **Her solo version first.** Test it on Patryk's phone before any shared database exists.
2. **The together layer on top of that.**

**Her solo game**
- She gets ALL of Ulhu's features, kept as close to his as possible. Only the teaching parts change.
- **Removed:** marking golems, book-marking and key-homework rituals and quests, planner links, school rules, term dates, Week A/B and INSET.
- **Renamed:** the lesson-ahead quest line becomes a general "get ahead" quest she points at her own thing. One wording pass swaps school words (pupils, lessons, marking) for general ones.
- **New for her:**
  - Starter packs at first launch: Fitness, Study, Home, Health, Creative, Blank.
  - Make-a-boss: she picks a name, a sprite and a target (e.g. "30 Home rituals"), or uses a ready-made preset (Laundry Kraken, Inbox Hydra).
  - A much bigger sprite library to support make-a-boss. This is its own build item.
- **Her own rest days and quiet hours.** No nudges after a time she picks, with 7pm suggested.
- **First week:** everything is on from day 1. The first time she meets each feature, Ulhu explains what it is once, in his devilish voice. This does not break the "no hints" rule: explaining what something is is allowed, and telling her what to do stays banned.
- **Time a day:** it must work both as a 2-minute tick-and-go and as a longer look around.

**Together layer**
- **Promises (the accountability):** each player picks up to 3 rituals as promises to the other, changed only once a week on a set day.
  - A broken promise shows the friend a grey mark and slows the shared boss.
  - It NEVER takes health, runes or progress from the friend's own game.
  - When a promise breaks, the app suggests sending a nudge. The friend chooses; it is never sent automatically.
- **What each sees of the other:** names only for rituals a player chooses to share. Everything else shows as a count ("5 of 7 today"). Health and other private things stay private unless shared.
- **First-build features:**
  - Shared boss: both players' rituals hit one health bar.
  - Weekly joint goal, e.g. "40 rituals between us", which pays both players.
  - One-tap nudge or blessing that lands on the other person's Call screen. Up to 10 a day each, never during the other person's quiet hours.
  - Kudos on each other's finished rituals, plus a small feed ("she beat the Swamp Wraith").
- **Later:** lair visits and gifting cosmetics.
- **Pause:** either player can pause alone, with no yearly limit.
  - Their own promises and penalties freeze.
  - The friend sees an "away" badge.
  - The shared boss carries on at the active player's pace.
- **On Patryk's screen:** a small strip on his Call screen shows her level, her promises today and the shared boss bar. Tapping it opens a full "Together" tab. Hers mirrors this.
- **Group size:** built for a pair, structured so a small group (3 to 5) can join later.
- **Where shared data lives:** a free online database holding ONLY shared things (names, levels, boss health, promises, kudos, feed). Each player's full habit list stays in their own app. Patryk's school or pupil data never goes in it. No paid services.
