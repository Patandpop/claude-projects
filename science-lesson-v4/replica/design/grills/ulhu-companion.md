# Grilling: Goal 3, Ulhu as a life companion

Grilled 29 Oct 2026 with Patryk (5 rounds). Builds on replica/design/habits-v2-decisions.md (moon phases, Vespers, High Vespers "Who you are becoming", fainting and Rise) and big-goals-research.md section 3. The "Decisions" block is final; the rounds below show how each was reached (later rounds win).

## Decisions (ready to build, after Goals 1 and 2)

**Why Ulhu exists (in priority order):** 1) give Patryk something to open instead of scrolling in the evening (each open is one tap from a ritual); 2) let his habits sit alongside his workload so heavy weeks don't feel like failure; 3) a mirror of who he is becoming. No AI and no paid calls: anything that looks like life is fixed rules. Ulhu never touches data and never decides.

**Build order:** high-priority parts first. Suggested: (1) ill-day rule + day tags, (2) overnight adventure + finds, (3) "who I'm becoming" term page, (4) story acts and chapters, (5) sound, (6) focus carving (mock-ups first), (7) nudges + widget (need the installed-app enabler).

1. **Aliveness:** Ulhu has a small life on a fixed clock: he wanders, sleeps and goes on adventures.
2. **Daily adventure:** once Vespers is done, he sinks into the deep and comes back next morning with a find.
   - While he's away the ledge is empty, with ripples where he dived and a small lantern glowing until he's back. The app works exactly as normal at night (tick, add habits, Vespers).
   - The find: mostly a lore line or an odd object for a Sanctum shelf; now and then a few runes.
   - Bad day: he still goes and brings back something small or strange. A bad day never costs anything.
   - Builder needs a pool of finds (objects + lore lines) per act, written in the "never explained" style.
3. **Year-long story:** 6 half-term acts + a 7th act for the summer holiday. Christmas, Easter and half-term breaks each get a short interlude.
   - Chapters: one per school week, closed by High Vespers on Sunday. The chapter's stained-glass pane fills with that week's colours (blue blessings, red curses, gold rituals).
   - Told only through things (relics, carvings, objects that wash up). Never explained; no text walls.
4. **Workload:** only Patryk's own day tags. Ulhu does NOT read the planner and makes no workload suggestions. Heavy days change no rules; they only appear on the term page.
   - Starting tags (editable): Marking, Parents' evening, INSET, Report writing, Trip, Cover, Ill.
5. **"Who I'm becoming" term page:** the identities (Scholar, Steadfast and the rest from habits-v2) as stained-glass figures that fill in as votes build over the term. Underneath: "on your better days, these came first" from simple counting.
6. **Ill days:** tagging a day "Ill" also turns the moon dark (full rest). On Ill days curses are still logged but cost no health, so being ill can never cause a faint.
7. **Streak protection:** none. No Elder Sign ward, no "tide returns". Moon phases and Ill days are the protection.
8. **Sound:** only big moments: ticks, level-up, fainting, Ulhu returning from his adventure. No time-of-day music. Always respects phone on silent.
9. **After 7pm:** nothing reaches out (no nudges). Sounds from his own taps (ticks, Vespers) still play.
10. **Nudges:** at most one a day, at the time he usually ticks, naming something real ("Read: 24 days"). Never sad, never pressure, never after 7pm, each switchable off. Needs the installed-app enabler; so does a home-screen widget.
11. **Focus carving:** wanted. Ulhu makes something while Patryk focuses; leaving the app PAUSES it, never crumbles it. Patryk: "this animation better have iterations and variations, not just stone carving, you can make this uber cute." So: several crafts and variations (not only stone carving), cute in style; mock-ups with several options before building.
