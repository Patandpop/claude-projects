### The wall (keeping ahead)
- **Purpose:** shows how far ahead the lessons are built. Keeping ahead = the whole next week built; two weeks ahead is the bonus (same as the lesson-ahead quest line).
- **One piece per lesson** in the next 2 weeks (his lessons only). Complete = lesson built AND homework set.
- **Feeling: calm.** Ahead = quiet sea, glowing wall/window. Rewards, never nags.
- **Full wall/window:** glows and pays the lesson-ahead quest bonus once. No separate wall reward.
- **Tap a gap:** jump to that lesson in the planner; on the phone show day, period and class.
- **Mock-ups (5):** 3 stained-glass iterations (his favourite direction), 1 sea wall redone (days labelled, class names in the gaps), 1 tide gauge (water mark = days ahead, carved marks at 1 and 2 weeks).
- **Stained glass, built but no homework:** the piece is lit but incomplete; homework completes it. Explore two-part objects instead of plain panes: two halves of a moon, two halves of a star, or another object (one part = lesson built, other part = homework set), or a lit pane with dull unfinished lead until homework is set.

### Paper golems (marking)
- **Summoned through the same summoning circle as the bosses.** Never automatic: the planner suggests, he taps to summon.
- **Book marking:** suggestion "[class] books due" appears at the start of the week the marking is due (finish-by that Friday). 2 sittings per class per term by default, changeable per class.
- **Year 12/13:** their marking IS PAG marking. Same elder-scholar golem as planned for 12-13 (no separate PAG creature). Each time a practical is done the planner suggests a PAG golem for that class; finish-by = one week after the practical.
- **A sitting:** a chunk of paper tears off, red ticks spread over it, one health block breaks.
- **Beating one:** runes like a boss + a small trophy of it kept in the Sanctum.
- **Overdue:** nothing bad happens; it stays until beaten.
- **Where:** always in the Teaching area (row under the wall). Also in The Call, but only when the finish-by date is within the next 3 days (due Friday shows from Wednesday).
- **Looks:** year group sets the shape (7 baby, 8-9 older, 10-11 bigger with red-pen scars, 12-13 elder scholars). He picks a theme per class from about 8 ready-made themes (e.g. coral, ember, frost, moss, ink, storm, ash, gold).
- **Year 7 redone cute:** soft round bundle of paper, big shiny eyes, stubby legs (soot-sprite feel).
- **Mock-ups:** 3 versions of the Year 7 golem + 2 improved versions each for 8-9, 10-11 and 12-13, showing the tear-and-ticks damage.

### Stone inscriptions: DROPPED
- No writing onto the object, no carving on save.
- **Instead:** restyle the options under every add form so they are on-theme, each in its object's material: wax seals under the quest scroll, obsidian chips under the ritual, red stone under the boss, glass chips under curses and blessings.
