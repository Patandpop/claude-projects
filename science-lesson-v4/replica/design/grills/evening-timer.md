# Grilling: the "where did my evening go" timer

Started 29 Oct 2026. Source: FABLE-WORKPLAN.md, Big goals, Goal 2 extras.

## Decisions

Grilled 29 Oct 2026, 5 rounds. Ready to build.

**What it is:** a work timer that shows how long each task really takes, after school and in frees, so the biggest drain can be automated next. It must feel seamless: it mostly logs by itself.

**Jobs:** Marking, Building lessons, Admin, Emails, Setting homework, Personal. Marking, Building and Homework carry a class. Personal is logged as minutes only, no site names.

**Home PC (logs by itself):**
- A free local tool (e.g. ActivityWatch) sees which website/program is in front and maps it to a job (Gmail = Emails, a lesson PowerPoint = Building, etc.).
- Class guessed from the file/folder name (e.g. "Latent Heat 9AS"), else just the job.
- Saves ONLY job, class and minutes. Never window titles (they can hold pupil names).
- When it starts a new job, a small Little Ulhu pop-up says what it is tracking; one click un-confirms ("not that"). Silence = yes.
- After 7pm: keeps logging, Ulhu stays silent (no pop-ups); un-confirming moves to the report.
- 5 minutes with no mouse/keyboard = stop counting.

**Phone (for things the PC cannot see, e.g. paper marking):**
- A home-screen button showing his 3 likeliest jobs from that day's classes. Two-tap picker (job, then class) as the fallback.
- One shared timer across phone and PC; starting a new job stops the old one.
- A forgotten phone timer stops when the PC sees another job, or after 2 hours max, flagged "maybe" in the report. He can correct any time by hand.

**When it counts:**
- After school: from the end of his last lesson (from the timetable), no cut-off.
- In frees: only tasks set for a free by the planner task checklist (tabs redesign). When the free starts, the phone offers one tap "Start: book marking 9AS"; it stops itself when the free ends, or earlier on stop. Phone only at school, nothing installed on school PCs.
- Otherwise the school day is not tracked.
- One task across several sittings adds up to one total.

**Report (every two weeks):**
- A card on the day screen every second week, opening a full planner page.
- Hours per job and per class, biggest drain on top, one line suggesting what could be automated next. He decides.
- Task times feed back: when he sets the same task again, the setter suggests "last time this took about 2 frees". Suggests only.

**Ulhu:** a small reward on each evening with no work logged after 7pm. Late nights cost nothing. Never a punishment.

**Privacy:** saved only in a private Sheet in his Google account. Report and pop-ups only on his phone and home PC, never on the board view. Nobody else sees it.

**Notes for the builder:**
- Depends on the planner task checklist (tabs redesign) for the frees part, and on the day screen for the report card. Build the PC logging + report first if those are not ready.
- Installing the PC tool is Patryk's job; give him plain steps. Propose a starting website-to-job list he can edit.
- The phone home-screen button may need the installed-app enabler or a simple Android shortcut; pick the simplest free route.
- No paid services, no microphone, no automatic work after 7pm.
