# Ulhu's 50 level titles (reference sheet)

**Status (31 Oct 2026):** these 50 titles were written in the visual grill of 28 Oct, approved by Patryk, and are already built into the game (`TITLES` and `DEPTHS` in `planner/game-ui.js`, the carved stone plaque). This sheet is the one place that lists them with what each one evokes, so any future screen, sound or line can match the mood of the title. It changes nothing in the game. A lore line per title was NOT chosen (28 Oct), so the "evokes" lines below are design notes, not text to show.

**Shape of the climb:** five depths of ten levels. Each new depth (levels 11, 21, 31, 41) gets its own special moment on top of the level-up rite. The tier thresholds line up with the depths: Nice stock from 5, Fancy from 12, Eldritch from 20 (Deep Acolyte), and one masterpiece per keeper at 30 (High Priest), 40 (Great Dreamer) and 50 (Great Old One). The plaque shows the Roman numeral, the title, and the depth name under it.

## Optional swaps (an offer, not a change)
